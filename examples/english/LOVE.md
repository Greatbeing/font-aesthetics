## Scheme 1: The O Is a Living Heart ★ Hero Scheme

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Core metaphor** | The O itself is a heart — not a symbol of one |
| **Color** | Deep crimson and molten red on near-black |
| **Composition** | The heart-shaped O glows at the center; the other letters lean toward it |
| **Imagery interpretation** | The word 'LOVE' is set in a bold classical serif, ALL CAPS, huge and centered. The letter O is formed as a living heart — the top of the O dips inward and the bottom comes to a point, a true heart shape, glowing deep crimson and molten red, as if lit from within. Concentric pulse ripples radiate outward from the heart-O, like a heartbeat made visible. The letters L, V, and E are in dark red-black, solid and monumental, leaning slightly toward the glowing heart. The background is near-black, so the heart is the only light in the frame. You understand it at a glance: the O is a heart, and the heart is beating. |

## What changed vs. the original five schemes

The original five (One Continuous Line, The Ring That Holds, Lowercase Whisper,
Two Weights One Word, The V as Open Arms) were all quiet, restrained images — whispers,
lightness, open arms. The user rejected them as too weak to carry a poster and asked for
elemental images with maximum contrast where the letterform IS the metaphor. Each new
scheme has exactly one overwhelming image, and the word is the image itself.
